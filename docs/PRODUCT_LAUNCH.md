# Introducing Autonomous Revenue Core

## Website intelligence. Explainable opportunity. Owner-controlled action.

![Autonomous Revenue Core](../assets/arc-hero.svg)

**Product-development announcement | Created by Derek Kupsky | October 9, 2026**

Autonomous Revenue Core (ARC) is a new website-sales intelligence tool in development. It is designed to bring business discovery, website analysis, explainable opportunity ranking, private prospect reports and personalized outreach drafts into one owner-controlled workflow.

The initial private plugin foundation is complete and configured **PAUSED**. This announcement introduces the product direction and foundation. The production research backend, Site, CRM, workers and live sending integrations remain pending; the product is not being presented as a generally available operating platform.

## The product promise

ARC is designed to answer three practical questions: Which businesses need a better website? What evidence supports that assessment? What should the owner do next?

Its planned research spans 63 criteria across business eligibility, design/mobile usability, performance/SEO/conversion, site structure, public business activity and capacity signals, and contact/action readiness. An explainable score combines website need (50%), ability to spend (25%), business activity (15%) and contactability (10%).

![Research-to-outcome flow](../assets/research-data-flow.svg)

Stored evidence is intended to support the lead score, private findings page, draft and CRM history. Every initial message requires approval of the actual recipient and content. The design keeps pause, suppression, cost limits and sending permissions separate from score thresholds.

## Availability and next stage

The private foundation and public project materials are available. Operational integrations are the next build stage. Planned pilot limits are 100 raw candidates/day, a target of five qualified leads and a hard $10/day paid research/API ceiling including model calls. These figures are caps and goals, not delivered output or the total cost of running the business.

No live customer results, revenue, conversion rate or ROI is claimed. Research and sends remain disabled until the required implementation, connection checks and separate activation.
