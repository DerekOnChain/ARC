# Autonomous Revenue Core

### Website intelligence. Explainable opportunity. Owner-controlled action.

**A product-development case study by Derek Kupsky.** ARC is designed to transform permitted public business and website evidence into prioritized website-sales opportunities, private findings reports and personalized outreach drafts.

**Foundation 0.1.0: complete and configured PAUSED.** The production research runtime, Site/backend connection, CRM and email integrations remain pending. The operating workflows below are planned capabilities; no live customer or revenue performance is claimed.

[Product announcement](docs/PRODUCT_LAUNCH.md) · [Read the full case study](docs/CASE_STUDY.md) · [Explore 63 research criteria](docs/RESEARCH_PARAMETERS.md)

## Product thesis

A weak website alone does not make a good lead. ARC combines observed website need, business spending signals, current activity and credible contact routes into an explainable opportunity assessment. The owner inspects the evidence and approves every initial message.

| Product layer | Intended role |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Find relevant businesses through authorized sources. |
| Website intelligence | Review design, mobile usability, performance, SEO, conversion and rebuild complexity. |
| Business intelligence | Corroborate operation, scale and public spending signals. |
| Explainable ranking | Show four score dimensions, evidence, confidence and unknowns. |
| Private prospect experience | Reuse research for a tailored findings page and short draft. |
| Command center | Manage leads, settings, costs, pause, approvals and outcomes. |

## An explainable scoring model

![Four-dimension score composition](assets/scoring-model.svg)

| Dimension | Weight | What it represents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Website need | 50% | Larger observed design, usability and conversion gaps score higher. |
| Ability to spend | 25% | Public capacity signals, with uncertainty; no claim of private finances. |
| Business activity | 15% | Contemporary evidence of legitimate operation. |
| Contactability | 10% | Credible business routes; generic inboxes are eligible. |

**70+** dashboard qualification · **80+** deep-research preference · **90+** initial outreach-review focus. These configurable defaults guide review and never authorize a send.

## Architecture at a glance

![Proposed ARC system architecture](assets/system-architecture.svg)

The assistant and command center are designed to share authenticated tools and durable backend state. A plugin foundation supplies instructions and context; continuous research, data persistence and sending require separately implemented backend services.

## Pilot design

At most **100 raw candidates/day**, a target of **five qualified leads**, and a hard **$10/day paid research/API cap** including model calls. These are planned limits, not delivered output or total business operating cost. The system must stop at the first reached limit and may return fewer qualified leads.

## Project status

The private foundation is complete. Research, schedules, live sending and follow-ups are disabled, with no autonomous runtime provisioned by that release. Site creation and backend connection are the next implementation stages; activation requires a separate owner instruction.

This public repository is a project showcase. It contains sanitized product documentation and diagrams, rather than proprietary runtime source, private prompts, credentials, internal conversations or prospect data. Product claims are scoped to the reviewed foundation record and supplied design specifications as of October 9, 2026.
